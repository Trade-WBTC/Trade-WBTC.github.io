Trade WBTC FAQ

How do I trade wbtc safely?

Open the official app domain, verify the WBTC contract, review the quote and price impact, and sign only after your wallet shows the expected assets.

Can I trade wbtc on Ethereum?

Yes. WBTC is an ERC-20 token on Ethereum; the mainnet contract is listed on Etherscan.

Do I need BTC to trade wbtc?

No. You can swap from supported Ethereum assets; minting or redeeming BTC through WBTC merchants is a separate process from a DEX swap.

Is trade wbtc the same as buying Bitcoin?

No. WBTC is a wrapped representation of BTC on Ethereum, so it carries token, custodian, liquidity, and wallet risks in addition to BTC price exposure.

What should I check before I trade wbtc?

Check the URL, wallet network, token address, route, price impact, approvals, and that you have enough ETH for gas.
